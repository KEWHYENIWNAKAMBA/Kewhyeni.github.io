I work at the intersection of intellectual property, entrepreneurship, and creative storytelling — building systems that help innovators and creators protect, structure, and scale their ideas globally.

My work combines legal frameworks, innovation strategy, and digital tools to transform ideas into structured intellectual assets and real-world impact.

Through research, strategic initiatives, and thought leadership, I aim to democratize access to IP systems for creators and entrepreneurs everywhere.
